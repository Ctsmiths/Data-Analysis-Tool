- Updated user guide for this release.

## v10.0.17 - 2026-09-22
- Added GitHub-based auto-update: the tool now checks for new versions on open and lets you know when one's available.
- Added a "Check for Updates" button and a "Download Fresh Copy" button to the About screen.
